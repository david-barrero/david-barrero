### 🏆 Best in Show, ASA DataFest 2026 (UGA)
**Who Gets Lost in Recovery?** With a five-person team, I analyzed 7M+ patient encounters from Stormont Vail Health to track fracture patients' recoveries. We found that patients who stay engaged with the health system (married, or using MyChart) had *more* recorded complications, because they come back to get them diagnosed, while disconnected patients drop out. I helped scope the analysis, interpreted the chi-squared and logistic regression results, and presented to the judges. *(Code is in a private team repo.)*
